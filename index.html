About Solis Consulting

We Build Relationship Technology

Every opportunity begins with a connection — between people, organizations, talent, and markets. Yet the information behind those connections is often fragmented, outdated, or difficult to act on.

Solis Consulting builds the infrastructure to make those relationships visible and useful.

Our core technology, RT Engine, is the Relationship Technology platform. It sources, verifies, and structures organizational information into connected intelligence. It helps teams understand where talent moves, how institutions evolve, and which opportunities deserve attention.

From collegiate recruiting and athletics to university careers and frontier AI, our applications turn complex relationship data into practical decisions.

One engine. Multiple applications. Built around the relationships that drive opportunity.
