About Solis Consulting

We Build University Systems That Work: The RT Engine

Universities run on thousands of moving pieces — student pathways, recruiting systems, employer pipelines, and institutional decisions that rely on real data. Our RT Engine brings all of that into one adaptive intelligence layer.

The RT Engine continuously gathers, verifies, and structures information from across the student and labor ecosystem. It identifies patterns, updates in real time, and turns raw signals into something every team can use: clear insight, faster planning, and workflows that finally feel modern.

We build systems that learn alongside your institution — powering recruiting, career services, and academic strategy with the same engine. One foundation, multiple products, built to make universities operate with precision instead of guesswork.
