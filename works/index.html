RT for Orgs

Human Data. RT for Orgs turns open student data into actionable insight. By analyzing data directories, Social activity, and intent signals, it builds precise candidate maps that help organizations recruit with focus and integrity.

Signal Systems. Our AI pipelines convert raw online presence into structured intelligence. RT for Orgs identifies leadership patterns and motivation, enabling outreach that feels human, not robotic.

Frictionless Growth. What took months of manual discovery now takes hours. RT for Orgs filters thousands of records instantly, surfacing the right people, right away.

Athletics

RT for Athletics

Roster Intelligence. RT for Athletics turns performance, market, and financial data into a live model of the roster. It gives decision-makers one place to understand player value, capital allocation, and emerging roster needs—connecting what happens on the field directly to management decisions.

Player Valuation. As new performance evidence enters the system, RT identifies where a player’s modeled value may have changed. Staff can inspect the evidence, confidence, risk, and valuation range behind each signal before deciding whether to act.

Roster Strategy. RT connects individual player decisions to the economics of the full roster. Staff can test moves, compare allocation scenarios, and understand the downstream impact of each decision—helping programs deploy limited roster capital with greater discipline and clarity.

Careers

RT for Careers

Market Intelligence. RT for Careers delivers real-time visibility into early-career hiring. Instead of relying on fragmented job postings or surveys, it uses AI-driven enumeration to identify and validate actual opportunities across employers and systems—creating a live, auditable model of true hiring capacity.

Adaptive Awareness. The platform monitors employer behavior to detect shifts in hiring activity across regions and sectors. Each update strengthens a persistent system memory, giving universities a clear, evolving understanding of labor market dynamics—not static or lagging snapshots.

Workforce Clarity. RT for Careers quantifies the gap between graduate output and verified demand, revealing the real absorptive capacity of the market. It replaces noise and duplication with defensible insight—enabling institutions to plan, advise, and allocate resources with precision.
