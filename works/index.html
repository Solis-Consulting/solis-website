RT for Access

Enterprise talent intelligence

Talent discovery. RT for Access helps organizations discover talent through the people, institutions, and relationships around a market. Teams see who matters, how they connect, and which opportunity is close enough to act on.

Company maps. A company is a market. Offices sit on orbits. Open a region, then an office, then the people on a team. Palantir is the first company map.

A region, as a demonstration. North Carolina’s richest 500 shows the same engine on a place rather than a company. It is searchable and grouped by industry, built from displayed estimates rather than a static directory.

AthleticsActive development

RT for Athletics

Roster and talent strategy

Roster intelligence. RT for Athletics turns performance, market, and financial relationships into a live model of the roster. Decision-makers see player value, capital allocation, and emerging roster needs in one place.

Player valuation. As new performance evidence enters the system, RT identifies where a player’s modeled value may have changed. Staff can inspect the evidence, confidence, risk, and valuation range behind each signal before deciding whether to act.

Roster strategy. RT connects individual player decisions to the economics of the full roster. Staff can test moves, compare allocation scenarios, and understand the downstream impact of each decision.

RT for Orgs

Organizational recruitment

The people who fit. RT for Orgs turns scattered student information into a ranked map of who belongs in the organization, and who to contact first.

Relationship signals. Leadership, intent, and public activity become a structured picture of a candidate, so outreach is specific to the person rather than a generic blast.

A shorter search. What took months of manual discovery now takes hours. The list surfaces the right people, ready for a decision.

CareersFuture expansion

RT for Careers

Workforce intelligence

Where opportunity sits. RT for Careers shows early-career hiring as a live map of real opportunities, not a pile of postings. Teams can see which employers are actually hiring and where talent can move.

A current picture. Hiring activity shifts by region and sector. Each update keeps the map current, so planning is based on what the market is doing now.

Talent and demand. RT for Careers makes the gap between available talent and real demand visible, so teams can advise and allocate with a clearer view of the opportunity.
